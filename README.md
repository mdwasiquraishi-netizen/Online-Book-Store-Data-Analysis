# Online-Book-Store-Data-Analysis
## 1) Introduction
The Online Book Store Dashboard is an interactive Power BI data analytics and business intelligence project designed to analyze book sales, customers, orders, inventory, genres, authors, and geographical performance.
The dashboard converts raw online bookstore data into meaningful visual insights through multiple analytical pages:
1.Overview
2.Sales Analysis
3.Book Analysis
4.Customer Analysis
5.Geography Analysis
6.Insights
The project helps stakeholders understand sales performance, customer behavior, book and genre performance, inventory levels, monthly trends, and geographical sales distribution. Interactive slicers and page navigation allow users to explore the data dynamically.

## 2) Dataset Information
The dataset represents an online bookstore's transactional, customer, book, and geographical information.

Main Dataset Attributes
Category	Important Fields
Book Information- Book Title, Genre, Author, Published Year
Sales Information- Sales, Quantity, Order Value
Inventory	Stock
Customer Information- Customer Name, Country, City
Order Information- Order Date, Month, Orders
Geography- Country, City
Classification- Genre, Author

## 4) Detailed Observation
1.The bookstore generated approximately 75.63K in total sales from 500 orders and approximately 3K books sold.
2.The average order value is approximately 151.26, indicating that customers generate meaningful revenue per transaction.
3.May records the highest monthly sales at approximately 8.3K.
4.Sales are not evenly distributed across the year. The difference between high-performing and low-performing months suggests an opportunity to investigate seasonality, promotions, customer demand, and marketing campaigns.

## 5) Recommendations
## 1. Focus on High-Performing Genres
   Romance
   Mystery
   Science Fiction
   Non-Fiction
   Fantasy
## 2. Investigate Low-Sales Months
   *June has considerably lower sales than the strongest months.
   *The business should consider:
     Seasonal promotions
     Discount campaigns
     Bundle offers
     Email marketing
     New-book launches
     Genre-specific offers during weaker periods.
## 3. Develop Customer Loyalty Programs
    High-value customers should be targeted with:
    *Loyalty points
    *Personalized discounts
    *Exclusive offers
    *Personalized book recommendations
    *Repeat-purchase incentives
## 4. Optimize Inventory
    Inventory should be linked with sales velocity.
    Books with:
       High Sales + Low Stock → Reorder quickly
       Low Sales + High Stock → Consider promotions
    This can help reduce inventory carrying costs.
## 5. Use Geographical Marketing
   Since customers are spread across many countries and cities, marketing campaigns can be segmented geographically.
    For example:
     Country-specific promotions
     Localized recommendations
     Region-based campaigns
     Shipping incentives
     Regional bestseller lists
## 6. Increase Cross-Selling
  Customers purchasing books from high-performing genres can be recommended similar books.
  For example:
    Romance buyer → Romance + Fantasy recommendations
    Mystery buyer → Mystery + Thriller recommendations
## 7. Analyze Customer Lifetime Value
   Future versions of the dashboard should include:
    Customer Lifetime Value
    Repeat Purchase Rate
    Customer Retention Rate
    New vs Returning Customers
    Customer Acquisition Cost
